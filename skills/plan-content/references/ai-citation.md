# AI citation rules for scoring

- In August 2026 ChatGPT's background searches cited listicles about half as
  often and comparison pages about a third less. How-to guides and original
  data held on every assistant. This lowers ease, not the page's place in the plan.
- Plan one dated statistics post per plan: a maintained stats page earns
  about four times the links of an average page.
- One target query per post. Its fan-out sub-questions become H2s, not
  separate posts.
- Answer blocks: a 40 to 60 word direct answer under each H2 phrased as the
  question people ask.
